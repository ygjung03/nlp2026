So, now I have confessed that he is thine
And I myself am mortgaged to thy will,
Myself I'll forfeit, so that other mine should be;
And I will, too, take thy trust from him;
And then I should, in the best way, be my own heir,
For the true heir will be mine by my own will,
That I might have him for a true heir
But I shall not have the true heir,
For the true heir shall hold my trust by the trust of his
Which shall hold mine by thy hand,
For mine is mine, my is yours, and thou art my.
  Now it is with this trust and the true heir

In faith, I do not love thee with mine eyes,
For they in thee a thousand errors note;
But 'tis my heart that loves what they despise,
As to me thou wilt see my true beauty:

11
Lo, as a careful huswife runs to catch
One of her feathered creatures broke away,
Sets down her babe, and makes all swift dispatch.
She looks to thee for help for a little.
Then she turns back again, and calls for thee,
And thou shalt be her husband's mistress.
Thy love's name is that of her love, and I know,
That I will not forget it.
